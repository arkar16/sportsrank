# Conference pages follow-up roadmap

1. **Monte Carlo conference simulations.** The owner’s high-priority favorite is a 10,000-trial simulation of the remaining season. Estimate odds of reaching and winning the football championship game or earning a share of the basketball regular-season title. Model league tiebreakers and extend the simulation to postseason and bid probabilities.
2. **Conference history pages.** Add a season-by-season table and a static SVG chart of mean CORS and conference rank at `/{sport}/conferences/{slug}/index.html`.
3. **Season-wide conference comparisons.** Add `/{sport}/conferences/years/{year}.html` and link it from each season page.
4. **Played SOS and Remaining SOS.** Show the strength of opponents played and scheduled in columns alongside team results.
5. **Biggest Conference Upsets and Upcoming Conference Games.** Add both sections to conference season pages.
6. **Postseason summaries.** Show NCAA tournament bids and combined tournament records for basketball, and bowl and College Football Playoff records for football.
7. **Wins vs. Expected.** Show how far a team's win total differs from the model's expectation.
8. **Mobile layout polish.** Improve the wide standings tables on small screens and consider a top-level Conferences masthead link.
