# Preserve every published artifact

Every published weekly and final ranking, spread, result, and supporting page will remain publicly addressable indefinitely at one canonical URL with its last-update timestamp. Corrected upstream data may update the page at that URL; immutable Release archives retain earlier revisions. Storage and release-generation mechanisms may change, but migrations must preserve the complete public record and URL compatibility rather than retaining only final rankings or recent seasons.
