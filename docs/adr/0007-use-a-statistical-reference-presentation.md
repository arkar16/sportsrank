# Use a statistical-reference presentation

SportsRank will present the model's generated results as a barebones statistical reference: direct navigation, readable tables, restrained styling, and minimal progressive enhancement. The site will avoid application-like presentation and editorial embellishment so the published artifact remains an inspectable representation of the model output.

For college football, the visual reference is the sports section of a printed
newspaper. Use clear season headings, compact rows, aligned figures and school
marks that help readers find their team. Density serves comparison. On a phone,
readers should still be able to find a team, read its movement and open its history.
Use the same typography, table styling and team links across the college football
site, including archived rankings, matchups and performance reports. A reader
following a team back through the archive should recognize the same publication.
