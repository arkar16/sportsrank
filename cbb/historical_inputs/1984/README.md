# 1983–84 men’s Division I basketball

The 275 team IDs use the March Mania program directory so teams retain their ratings when the replay crosses into 1984–85. The 275 programs in the regular-season score file match the year’s conference standings, including the independents, after accounting for school name changes. [Season and conference tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983%E2%80%9384_NCAA_Division_I_men%27s_basketball_season)

The compact results contain 3,633 regular-season and conference tournament games, 52 NCAA tournament games and 32 NIT games. The date column counts days since October 31, 1983. The site column gives home, away or neutral from the winner’s perspective. The dated file has 3,633 individual scores even though the source’s separate count summary lists 3,632. Each dated pairing occurs once. [Dated results](https://academics.smcvt.edu/jtrono/NCAA_Archive/83_84_dts.htm) · [NCAA results](https://academics.smcvt.edu/jtrono/NCAA_Archive/83_84_ncaa.htm) · [NIT results](https://academics.smcvt.edu/jtrono/NCAA_Archive/83_84_nit.htm)

Postseason dates come from matching season, competition, both teams and scores against school game histories. The NCAA bracket supplies the remaining opening-round games and the dates for Louisville–Morehead State and Memphis–Oral Roberts. Louisville won 72–59 on March 16. The bracket and Louisville’s game history agree on the score. [Pinned game histories](https://github.com/pollackelliott/college-basketball-history/blob/8189b49651171b032d1c1d19f54f0d39ee1ed675/data/canonical/games.csv) · [NCAA brackets, 1984 on PDF page 39](https://s3.amazonaws.com/fs.ncaa.org/Docs/stats/m_final4/Brackets.pdf#page=39)

Lamar’s schedule supplies its two NIT dates. Louisiana’s tournament record confirms all five of its NIT games under its former Southwestern Louisiana name.
