Scoring rules

Only games between Division I teams count. Each margin uses the Monday ratings preceding tipoff. CORS line coverage is the percentage of favorites that beat the CORS spread, excluding pushes. An even spread (pick’em) has no winner or coverage pick. Mean absolute error (MAE) and root mean square error (RMSE) include all evaluated games, including pick’ems. Lower margin error is better.
